Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

1. What this policy covers

We use cookies and similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, to make the site work and, only if you agree, to understand how it is used and to measure advertising.

2. Always on (essential), no consent needed

Name Type Purpose Duration
electricink_cart Local storage Keeps your basket. Stays until you complete a purchase or clear it.
electricink_last_order Local storage Shows your order summary after payment. Removed when you open the confirmation page.
ei_cookie_consent Local storage Remembers your cookie choice. We ask again after about 6 months.
__stripe_mid Cookie (Stripe) Set by Stripe on the checkout page to process payments and prevent fraud. Up to 1 year
__stripe_sid Cookie (Stripe) Set by Stripe on the checkout page to process payments and prevent fraud. 30 minutes

3. Only if you press "Accept all"

Type Name Purpose Duration
Analytics: Google Analytics 4 _ga and _ga_<container id> They help us understand which pages are visited. They identify a browser with a pseudonymous ID. Up to 2 years
Marketing: Meta Pixel _fbp They help us measure the results of our advertising. Up to 90 days
Marketing: Meta Pixel _fbc They help us measure the results of our advertising. Up to 90 days, only if you arrive from a Meta ad link

Durations are those stated by each provider.

4. Other services the site loads without cookies

Google Fonts (your IP address is sent to Google to load the fonts), Vercel Speed Insights (measures page performance), and our own servers hosted on Vercel and Railway. These do not set cookies and are needed to show and run the site.

5. Your choices

When you first visit, you can press Accept all or Reject all. You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie preferences link in the footer. If you reject, analytics and marketing cookies are deleted and no longer load. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

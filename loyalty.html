How it works

Simple. Automatic. Yours.

Programme terms

The small print.

Questions? hello@electricink.ie — Last updated March 2026.

  • Catokens are earned on VAT-inclusive order totals. Earn rate: 4/€1 at Fresh Ink, up to 10/€1 at Black Cat.
  • Catokens are awarded after successful payment — not on pending or failed orders.
  • Ranks are based on lifetime Catokens from purchases and missions only. Referral Catokens do not count toward rank.
  • Rank discounts of 3% (Legend) and 5% (Black Cat) apply to consumables only, via coupon at checkout.
  • Coupons are valid for 30 days. Unused coupons are automatically refunded in Catokens.
  • The rewards shop rotates. Electric Ink Ireland may add, remove, or change rewards at any time.
  • Referral bonus of 1,000 Catokens is awarded once per referred customer, on their first order of €75+. Self-referrals are not permitted.
  • The ongoing 10% referral commission is awarded automatically on every qualifying order by a referred customer.
  • The referred customer discount of €15 is valid on first orders of €75+ and cannot be combined with other offers.
  • Monthly missions reset on the 1st at midnight (Dublin time). Incomplete missions do not carry over.
  • Surprise missions are triggered at Electric Ink Ireland's discretion. Duration and multiplier may vary.
  • Catokens have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and are subject to Irish consumer law and GDPR.

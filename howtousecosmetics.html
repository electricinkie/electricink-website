Professional Tattoo Care

How To Use Electric Ink Cosmetic Line

The entire Electric Ink cosmetic line is made with natural raw materials and contains no animal-derived ingredients commonly found in industrial cosmetics, ensuring a completely vegan formulation.

Phase 1

Pre-Tattoo Care

Skin Scrub Out

1

Exfoliating gel for the first step of prep. Clears dead skin and excess keratin without drying, and softens the hair before you shave.

  • Apply to the area before shaving
  • Massage in small circles to exfoliate
  • Remove the particles with Tattoo Prep or rinse with water
Shop Skin Scrub Out

Tattoo Prep

2

Second step of prep. Shaving foam with Aloe Vera and Chamomile, to use instead of alcohol: it cleans the skin, removes leftover exfoliant and lets the razor glide.

  • Apply the foam directly on the skin
  • Spread it and shave the area
  • Wipe off the excess with paper, no rinsing needed
Shop Tattoo Prep
Phase 2

Stenciling

Transfer IT

3

Stencil cream with extra hold for thermal and hand-drawn stencils. Stays sharp through long sessions and only comes off with IT itself.

  • Apply a thin layer on clean, dry skin and spread until a transparent film forms
  • Place the stencil and press firmly for about 10 seconds
  • If the stencil is misplaced, rub a little IT over it before it dries and wipe it off
  • Let it dry completely before you start
Shop Transfer IT

Stencil Fix

4

Spray that sets stencils and freehand drawings. Use it after Transfer IT: it removes excess carbon and keeps the lines from smudging.

  • Apply the stencil (after Transfer IT) or draw freehand
  • Spray Stencil Fix onto a paper towel
  • Press the paper onto the skin for 5 seconds
Shop Stencil Fix

The Gloo

5

Stencil transfer gel for printed stencils. Dries fast and holds well on complex areas like elbows, knees and joints.

  • Apply a thin, even layer on clean, dry skin
  • Position the stencil with no bubbles or wrinkles and press for 10 to 15 seconds
  • Peel off the paper slowly
  • Let it dry before you start
Shop The Gloo
Phase 3

During the Tattoo

Tattoo Clean Up

6

Cleans the skin during the session without disturbing the stencil. pH-balanced and hydrating, with a fresh feel.

  • Apply the foam directly on the skin and spread it
  • Wipe with a paper towel until all impurities are gone
  • No rinsing needed
Shop Tattoo Clean Up

Vaseline Slip

7

Pure paraffin vaseline of pharmaceutical grade. The simple glide: smooth needle movement, less resistance on the skin, and a little goes a long way.

  • Once the stencil is on and fixed, take a little with a spatula
  • Spread a thin layer over the skin
  • Reapply whenever needed
Shop Vaseline Slip

Vaseline Slip Premium

8

The enriched Slip: pure paraffin with clove and macadamia oils, omegas and vitamins E and C, for extra hydration while you work.

  • Once the stencil is on and fixed, take a little with a spatula
  • Spread a thin layer over the skin
  • Reapply whenever needed
Shop Vaseline Slip Premium

Cat Slobber

9

Vegan butter made for the needle glide. Hydrates and softens, with a mild fragrance in Traditional, Mint or Clove & Cinnamon.

  • Once the stencil is on and fixed, take a little with a spatula
  • Spread a thin layer over the skin
  • Reapply whenever needed
Shop Cat Slobber

Slimer Gel

10

Water-based glide for the whole session. Replaces vaseline or butter, wipes away excess ink and leaves a clean shine for photos.

  • Apply with a spatula as your glide, once the stencil is on and fixed
  • Mid-session: wipe away excess ink, or leave a layer on areas you've finished
  • At the end: a final layer for a fresh, clean shine in photos
Shop Slimer Gel
Phase 4

After the Tattoo

For Professionals

Tattoo After All

11

The spray for the final clean-up, right after the tattoo. Leaves the skin clean and ready for the next aftercare step, no rinsing needed.

  • Spray over the finished tattoo and leave it for a few seconds
  • Press a paper towel lightly on the skin to take off the excess
  • Let the skin dry, then continue with Barrier Film or Protection
Shop Tattoo After All

Tattoo Barrier Film

12

A cream that turns into a transparent, breathable second skin on contact with air. A film for the fresh tattoo, with no need for cling film.

  • Start on clean, dry skin, after After All
  • Apply a thin layer and let it dry completely
  • To remove, wash with warm running water
Shop Tattoo Barrier Film

Tattoo Protection

13

The roll of transparent second skin: a breathable, waterproof film that stays on the fresh tattoo for up to 4 days.

  • Start on clean, dry skin, after After All, with gloves on
  • Cut the film with a safety margin and press it on without wrinkles
  • Leave it on for up to 4 days, replace only if a lot of fluid builds up
Shop Tattoo Protection

Tattoo Dry

14

For the end of the session: solidifies the liquid waste so it can be thrown away without spills.

  • Put the liquid waste in your container
  • Add one unit and let it set
  • Dispose of it following your studio's waste procedure
Shop Tattoo Dry
Phase 5

Home Care

For Clients

Tattoo Balm

The balm for the first two weeks. Moisturizes the fresh tattoo and absorbs fast.

  • Wash your hands and apply a thin layer over the tattoo
  • Use it 2 to 3 times a day for 14 days
  • Follow your tattooer's aftercare advice
Shop Tattoo Balm

Tattoo Cream Derma

The daily moisturizer for the tattoo once it has healed (10+ days).

  • Wait until the tattoo is healed (10+ days)
  • Apply a thin layer and rub in until absorbed
  • Use it daily
Shop Tattoo Cream

Clean Up

The same foam cleanser from the studio, now for the tattoo at home. Cleans the skin with no rinsing needed.

  • Apply the foam directly on the skin
  • Wipe with a paper towel until it is clean
  • No rinsing needed
Shop Clean Up

Important Aftercare Tips

Follow these guidelines for the best tattoo healing results

Keep It Clean

Wash the tattoo gently and keep it clean to prevent infections.

Moisturize Regularly

Apply Tattoo Balm/Tattoo Cream Derma daily to keep the tattoo hydrated and aid in healing.

Wash Hands First

Always wash your hands before touching your tattoo to avoid introducing bacteria.

Follow Artist's Advice

Stick to the specific aftercare instructions given by your tattoo artist for the best results.

Avoid Sun Exposure

Protect your tattoo from direct sunlight to prevent fading and damage.

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water to help your skin heal and keep your tattoo looking its best.
